Nutrition evidence assistant: research study

An AI assistant that answers nutrition and health questions from published research papers and shows its sources.

About this study

We are testing how well an AI assistant answers nutrition and health questions using scientific papers. You will ask up to 8 questions of your own, read each answer and its sources, and rate it. It takes about 10 to 15 minutes.

  • Anonymous. We do not ask for your name, email or any personal or health details, and we do not store your IP address. Please do not type personal information in your questions.
  • What we store: your questions, the answers, your ratings and comments, and the two optional background questions below, for research analysis and publication in aggregate.
  • Voluntary. You can stop at any time by closing the page.
  • Not medical advice. Answers are generated automatically from research abstracts, may be wrong or incomplete, and are not a substitute for a doctor or dietitian. In an emergency, contact local emergency services.
