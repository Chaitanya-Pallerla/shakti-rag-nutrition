Welcome to SHAKTI

Smart Health Advisor for Knowledge-based Tailored Interventions is a research prototype. By chatting with it you help us evaluate how relevant, trustworthy and useful its answers are.

  • Ask any health, diet or food question (up to 20). Each answer shows its sources; rate it with an emoji.
  • Participation is voluntary and anonymous. We store your questions, the answers, your ratings and your optional comments with a random ID only: no name, email or IP address. Please don't type personal details such as your name or medical record.
  • Answers are research information, not medical advice. Talk to a clinician about your own health.
